Ace Turtle Services Pvt Ltd (brand "ace turtle") is a Bengaluru, India retail technology company
founded in 2014. It runs a vertical commerce model for international fashion and lifestyle brands
and is the exclusive licensee in India and South Asia for Lee, Wrangler, G-STAR, Toys"R"Us and
Dockers. Its three proprietary platforms are Rubicon (omnichannel commerce: distributed order
management, inventory, catalogue, pricing and logistics management), Connect App (store operations)
and Albus (machine learning for inventory optimisation and dynamic markdown).
